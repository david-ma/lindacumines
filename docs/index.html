Linda Cumines

B Sc, Dip Nutrition and Dietetics, Accredited Practising Dietitian

Linda combines the latest scientific information with behavioural strategies to suit individual needs.

Her skill in critical health care is significant: she was the first cardiac transplant dietitian in Australia. She has the ability to communicate health advice clearly, previously writing a monthly column in the Australian Women’s Weekly.

Linda prefers face-to-face consultations, and home visits are also available. Out-of-hours appointments and home visits are possible.

Linda enjoys working collaboratively with her clients – individuals, families, organisations and companies – to cultivate positive mental and physical health outcomes through food.

Finding the right person to confide in with confidence and respect for your privacy is important. Linda is reliable, dynamic and honest.

After many years of clinical experience in hospital, media and private practise, Linda has a practical approach to ‘delivering the science’ in a way that enhances our relationship with food.

She educates through promoting healthy eating behaviours and strengthening food confidence and nutrition alertness. She is driven to explore the art of culinary nutrition. She is always in the home kitchen science lab, recipe testing, reformulating & nutritional modelling.

Through her concern about nutritious food choices for our ageing population, she has innovated Nutriloops™️ – Australia’s first aged-care-specific nutritious biscuit range. In addition to private practice, Linda consults to organisations and companies with a focus on providing appropriate nutrition solutions for ageing Australians.

Linda specialises in practical advice for healthy wellbeing, including weight management, diabetes, gastrointestinal and cardiac conditions.

Services

Weight management - Body composition, Weight inclusive care

Cardiac health - Cholesterol lowering, Hypertension
Diabetes - Pre diabetes, Insulin resistance, Type 2 Diabetes
Fertility & Pregnancy
Women’s health - Insulin resistance, PCOS Polycystic ovarian syndrome, Endometriosis, Menopause pre/post, Osteoporosis
